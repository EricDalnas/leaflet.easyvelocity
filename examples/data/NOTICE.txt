NOAA GFS sample data in this folder
===================================

Files:  gfs-20220928-{06,12,18}z-f000-10m-uv.grib2   (Hurricane Ian, 28 September 2022)
        gfs-20221223-{06,12,18}z-f000-10m-uv.grib2   (Winter Storm Elliott, 23 December 2022)
        gfs-20220928-0p50-10m-uv.nc                  (Hurricane Ian, NetCDF, derived; see below)

What they are: the 10 m above ground U and V wind messages (UGRD / VGRD) of the NOAA GFS
0.25 degree analysis (forecast hour 0) for the given dates and cycles, copied byte-for-byte from
the public NOAA bucket  https://noaa-gfs-bdp-pds.s3.amazonaws.com  (each file is just the two
original GRIB2 messages, one after the other; nothing was re-encoded or altered).

The NetCDF file is NOT unaltered NOAA data: it was converted from the three Ian GRIB2 files above
by tools/grib2-to-netcdf.js (--stride 2 --pack), keeping every second grid point (0.5 degree) and
rounding the values to 0.01 m/s (16-bit integers with scale_factor).

Attribution: "Wind data: NOAA/NCEP Global Forecast System (GFS)".

You can delete this folder; the example page then simply loses its "Archived" entries
(remove them from `datasets` in examples/index.html to avoid failed requests).
